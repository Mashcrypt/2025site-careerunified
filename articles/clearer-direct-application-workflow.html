A Clearer Direct Application Workflow for Recruiters

In this articleSet up your company profilePost jobs with screening questionsManage applications and candidatesCommunicate with candidates
Share this post

Bring job posts, screening questions, candidate records and communication into one focused workflow with Career Unified's recruiter platform.

Set up your company profile

Your company profile is the foundation of every job post. Set it once and we will auto-fill your job posts with your company details, logo, and contact information.

  • Company name, industry, size, and location.
  • Company email, website, and social media links.
  • Logo upload with automatic sync to existing job posts.
  • About section to help candidates understand your organisation.

Post jobs with screening questions

Create job posts that attract the right candidates and filter efficiently.

  • Five-step job wizard with validation at each step.
  • Add structured screening questions with essential criteria.
  • Choose Direct Apply or external application link.
  • Preview the job before publishing.

Manage applications and candidates

Review candidates, screening answers and submitted CVs in one place.

  • Pipeline stages: New, Contacted, Interview, Offer, Hired, Rejected.
  • Drag and drop candidate cards between stages.
  • Add private notes and tags for internal collaboration.
  • Talent pool with candidate consent tracking.

Communicate with candidates

Send emails directly from the platform with approved templates.

  • Email or Whatsapp individual candidates with job context.
  • Use approved templates for common updates.
  • View sent email history with delivery status.
  • Handle failed emails safely.

Ready to streamline your hiring?

Set up your company profile and start posting jobs with Direct Apply today.
