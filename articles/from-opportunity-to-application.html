From Opportunity to Application: A Practical Checklist

In this articleVerify the opportunityPrepare the documentsTrack your submission
Share this post

A successful application is rarely the result of one perfect template or one high score. It comes from understanding the opportunity, presenting relevant evidence clearly and following the correct application route.

Verify the opportunity

Before submitting personal information or documents, pause long enough to confirm that the opportunity and the application route make sense.

  • Read the complete vacancy description and identify the employer, location, closing date and minimum requirements.
  • Compare the application link with the employer's official careers page when one is available.
  • Prepare a CV that reflects experience and qualifications you can support with truthful evidence.
  • Follow the stated application method. A public-service post may require a Z83 form, while another employer may use an external portal or Career Unified Direct Apply.
  • Do not pay an informal fee, share passwords or send sensitive documents through an unverified contact.

Prepare the documents

Use each resource for the part of the process it is designed to support. Start with the official opportunity information, then prepare the documents and answers that the application actually requires.

  • For public service posts: complete the Z83 form and prepare a detailed CV.
  • For other employers: follow their stated application method and document requirements.
  • Keep certificates and academic records ready in case shortlisted candidates are asked for them.
  • Make sure your CV dates agree with the application form.

Track your submission

Keep a record of what you submitted, when, and to whom.

  • Save a copy of the completed application form and CV.
  • Note the reference number, closing date, and submission method.
  • If you applied by email, save the sent message and any auto-reply.
  • If you used a portal, take a screenshot of the confirmation.
