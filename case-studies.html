Case study 1: Preparing a public-service application

Applicant workflow

From a government vacancy to a checked Z83 package

A job seeker identifies a public-service vacancy that requires the official Z83 form and a detailed CV. The main risk is not the form itself, but transferring the wrong post information, missing a declaration or following generic advice instead of the vacancy instructions.

Starting point
The applicant has a specific vacancy title, department, reference number and closing date.
Career Unified flow
Review the vacancy, open the Z83 Filler, enter the post and personal information, generate the document, and compare it with the section-by-section Z83 guide.
Human checks
Confirm the official form version, signature requirement, submission channel and supporting-document instructions in the vacancy advert.
Practical value
A repeatable checklist reduces avoidable omissions and keeps the application tied to the correct post.

Case study 2: Using ATS feedback without inventing experience

Job seeker workflow

From a general CV to a role-focused application

A user has a complete CV and a job description, but the language in the CV does not clearly show the most relevant experience. The objective is to improve clarity and alignment once, without repeatedly spending AI credits or adding claims the user cannot support.

Starting point
A truthful CV, a selected job description and enough real experience or skills to assess against the role.
Career Unified flow
Run ATS analytics, review missing or weak evidence, convert relevant feedback into AI Tailor context, generate edits, and inspect the resulting CV before downloading.
Human checks
Verify dates, qualifications, employers, responsibilities, achievements and keywords. Remove any unsupported statement.
Practical value
The user receives a clearer role-focused draft and a structured explanation of remaining risks. The ATS result remains guidance, not a shortlist guarantee.

Case study 3: Receiving applications directly

Recruiter workflow

From recruiter account to a reviewable candidate pipeline

An employer wants a branded vacancy page and a consistent set of applicant details rather than receiving unstructured applications across unrelated channels.

Starting point
An accountable recruiter contact, company information, a logo and a complete vacancy description.
Career Unified flow
Create the recruiter account, prepare the vacancy through the five-step job workflow, choose direct apply, add role-relevant screening questions, review the confirmation page and publish.
Candidate flow
A signed-in candidate reviews the vacancy, supplies required information, submits a CV and answers the configured screening questions.
Recruiter flow
Applications appear in the recruiter workspace, where candidate information can be reviewed and moved through defined hiring stages.
Governance checks
Ask only necessary questions, protect applicant information, apply criteria consistently and keep the final hiring decision with the recruiter.

Limitations and evidence standards

  • Career Unified cannot guarantee employment, interviews, admission, funding, candidate quality or a particular ATS score.
  • AI assistance can improve wording and structure, but users must verify every generated statement.
  • Official employers, departments, universities and funders control their own requirements and decisions.
  • Recruiters must apply their own lawful hiring, retention and information-protection obligations.
  • Named customer outcomes should not be published without consent and supporting evidence.
