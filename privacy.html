Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us — transparency is our priority

Career Unified is an independent, third-party career discovery platform designed to help users find publicly available opportunities such as jobs, bursaries, universities, and career tools.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling information responsibly in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa and generally accepted data-protection principles such as transparency, minimal data collection, purpose limitation, and security safeguards.

This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the Career Unified website, tools, and content. It explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have.

This policy also covers how we handle data when you interact with platform features such as search, filters, analytics, and AI tools (where available). By using Career Unified, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

3. Data Controller (Responsible Party)

Career Unified is the responsible party (sometimes called the “data controller”) for the limited information processed through this platform. This means we determine the purpose and method of processing information in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable South African law.

If you have privacy questions or requests, you can contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

4. Information We Collect

Career Unified collects only limited information necessary to operate and improve the platform. Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Search terms and filters
  • Anonymous usage data
  • Device/browser technical data
  • CV information entered into the CV Generator

We do not require account registration and do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as ID numbers, banking details, or passwords through this page. If you choose to contact us directly, you may share information voluntarily (for example, in a support message).

5. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above strictly for legitimate business purposes aligned with POPIA, including operating the service, improving user experience, and maintaining platform safety. This includes:

  • Platform functionality:
  • Performance and reliability:
  • Analytics and improvement:
  • Security and abuse prevention:

6. CV Generator & Local Device Storage

The CV Generator operates entirely within your browser using local storage. This means your CV data is stored on your device and is not automatically transmitted to Career Unified.

  • Your CV data remains on your device
  • Career Unified does not receive, upload, or store your CV information
  • You are solely responsible for managing and protecting this data

Clearing your browser storage will permanently remove saved CV information. If you share or export your CV, you should take reasonable precautions (for example, sending it only to trusted recipients).

7. AI Tools & Data Processing (Including AI Tailor)

Career Unified may offer AI-powered tools (such as an AI Tailor) to help users generate or improve career-related content. When you use AI tools, the platform may process the information you provide in order to generate outputs.

We aim to follow privacy-by-design principles when offering AI features. Unless explicitly stated otherwise:

  • Real-time processing: AI inputs are processed to generate outputs for your use
  • No unnecessary storage: we avoid retaining AI inputs longer than needed for functionality
  • No selling of AI inputs: we do not sell AI inputs as data products

Please avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information into AI tools. You remain responsible for reviewing, editing, and deciding how to use AI-generated content.

8. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

Career Unified uses cookies and browser storage technologies to provide a smoother experience and to understand general site usage. These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember user preferences (such as cookie consent)
  • Improve platform performance and usability
  • Measure anonymous usage trends for improvement purposes

These technologies do not personally identify you on their own. You may disable cookies via your browser, but certain features may not function as intended.

9. Payments & Financial Information

Career Unified does not collect, store, or process your full banking or card information directly on this platform when payments are handled through third-party payment providers. Payment providers typically manage payment security, card storage, and payment verification.

We may receive limited payment-related confirmations (for example, that a payment succeeded or failed, or a transaction reference), but we do not receive full card details. The privacy practices of third-party payment providers are governed by their own policies.

We keep information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including operational, security, and legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and how it is processed.

15. POPIA Explained (Plain Language)

POPIA is South Africa's data-protection law that requires personal information to be collected and used in a lawful, transparent, and secure manner.

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Know what data is collected and why
  • Request access to personal information (where applicable)
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally permissible
  • Expect reasonable safeguards to protect information
