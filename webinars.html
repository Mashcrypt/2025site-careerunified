Who the programme is designed to serve

Job seekers

Application preparation

Practical sessions can help participants understand vacancy instructions, prepare a truthful CV, use feedback proportionately and identify the correct application route.

Schools, institutions and community programmes

Career-readiness support

A hosted session can focus on a defined group, such as final-year students, graduates, bursary applicants or people preparing public-service applications.

Recruiters and employers

Platform and workflow orientation

A walkthrough can demonstrate how Career Unified presents vacancies, receives direct applications and helps a recruiter organise candidate information.

Request or propose a session

When contacting Career Unified, include the organisation or group name, expected audience, approximate number of attendees, preferred topic, proposed date range and whether the session should be online or hosted.

Career Unified will confirm scope, availability, presenter details and any applicable terms in writing. Do not make a payment or circulate a registration link until it has been confirmed through an official Career Unified channel.
