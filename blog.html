A five-minute check before applying

Before submitting personal information or documents, pause long enough to confirm that the opportunity and application route make sense.

  • Read the complete vacancy description and identify the employer, location, closing date and minimum requirements.
  • Compare the application link with the employer's official careers page when one is available.
  • Prepare a CV that reflects experience and qualifications you can support with truthful evidence.
  • Follow the stated application method. A public-service post may require a Z83 form, while another employer may use an external portal or Career Unified direct apply.
  • Do not pay an informal fee, share passwords or send sensitive documents through an unverified contact.

ATS feedback is guidance, not a hiring promise

An applicant tracking system check can identify missing role language, weak section structure and information that may be difficult for software to read. It cannot confirm that a recruiter will shortlist or hire a candidate.

When Career Unified feedback is converted into AI Tailor edits, the resulting CV should still be reviewed by the user. Keep only accurate statements, retain the correct dates and employers, and remove any wording that overstates responsibility or results.

Responsible use: A stronger match should come from clearer evidence and relevant language, never invented qualifications, skills, duties or achievements.

Our editorial standards

Career Unified aims to make opportunity information easier to understand without presenting itself as the employer, university, funder or government department responsible for the final decision.

  • Official application pages and published vacancy instructions take priority.
  • Material changes should be reflected with a review date where practical.
  • Guides distinguish general preparation advice from mandatory application requirements.
  • We do not promise employment, admission, funding, interview selection or a particular ATS outcome.

Corrections and source concerns can be sent through the Career Unified contact page.
