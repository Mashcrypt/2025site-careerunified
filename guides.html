Use ATS and AI feedback responsibly

Compare your CV with the job description, identify missing relevant evidence and use AI Tailor only to improve truthful content. An ATS score is an indicator, not a hiring decision.

Responsible use of Career Unified

  • Applicants remain responsible for the accuracy of CVs, forms and screening answers submitted in their name.
  • Recruiters remain responsible for lawful, fair vacancy requirements and their final hiring decisions.
  • AI-generated wording must be reviewed and corrected before it becomes part of an application.
  • Fees, dates, eligibility rules and application channels should be confirmed with the relevant official organisation.
