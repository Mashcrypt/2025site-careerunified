The hiring journey

How Hiring
Becomes Effortless

From posting a job to making the final hire, keep every step clear and connected.

  1. Post your job in minutes

    Publish the role, required skills, experience level, and location. Create a branded career site without waiting for a developer, then add your company story, logo, social links, and open roles on your Career Unified subdomain.

  2. Step 02

    AI matches relevant candidates

    Compare applications with role requirements and surface relevant skills, experience, and qualifications for recruiter review.

  3. Step 03

    Review and interview

    Candidates reuse their Career Unified profile and CV, then answer job-specific questions. Review all three together and move applicants through shortlist and interview stages.

  4. Step 04

    Hire with confidence

    Keep private notes and stage updates with the application, coordinate the team, and send candidate outcome emails while your recruiters make every decision.
